## Původní zadání

Use case: style-transfer with identity preservation.
Asset type: transparent PNG portrait for the FMK student portfolio gallery.
Input image 1 is the identity and clothing reference, the actual portrait included in this portfolio project. Transform this same person into a detailed multicolor distressed rubber-stamp print. Preserve her recognizable face, natural facial features and freckles, expression, slight head angle, messy tied-up hair and stray curls, ordinary natural appearance, shoulder pose, T-shirt and knitted cardigan shapes, and tote strap. No beautification, slimming, age change, cartoon or invented facial features.

Deliver a SINGLE portrait image on real alpha transparency, vertical exact 4:5 ratio, ideally 1200 by 1500 pixels. Show the whole hair and head plus shoulders and upper chest. Reframe the supplied photograph so there is 6 to 9 percent clear space above the highest hair. Crown to chin should occupy 45 to 50 percent of total canvas height, with the eyes 30 to 35 percent from the top. Center the face horizontally. Keep original proportions and orientation. Do not crop off the hair.

Style: finely detailed rubber stamp or restrained relief-print illustration. Charcoal linework plus muted brick red, dark slate blue and forest green inks only. Convert the mustard cardigan into muted forest green while preserving its knitted texture and cut; pale fabric is mostly unprinted negative space. Preserve sufficient ink detail for eyes, nose, mouth and freckles to remain readable. Distressed imperfect ink edges, small missing ink flecks, delicate print grain, subtle fine hatch shadows. Limited solid ink patches. No glossy photographic skin, watercolor, comic-book outlines or plastic AI portrait.

Remove all background architecture, people, trees and location cues. Only a very few sparse abstract ink marks near the shoulders may remain. No landmarks, flags or invented place. Remove FMK lettering from the shirt; do not include any text, name, caption, number, logo, border, folder shape, paper rectangle, drop shadow or watermark. This image must sit directly on the gallery's own paper. All space around the printed portrait and the unprinted gaps must be genuinely transparent, never painted white or a drawn checkerboard. Save the final output as a PNG with alpha.
